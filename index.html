Questions

Things people often ask

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you share with your psychologist is confidential, within the limits of the law and professional ethics (for example, where someone's safety is at serious risk). Read our Privacy Policy for details.

How do online sessions work?

Choose a time on our booking page and pay securely online. We then send your private session link on WhatsApp. At your time, join by video or voice from your phone or computer.

How long is a session, and what does it cost?

Sessions are usually 45 to 50 minutes. Each psychologist's fee is shown on their profile, so you know it before you book.

Can I choose my psychologist and language?

Yes. You can tell us your concern, language and preferences, and we show psychologists who match. You choose who to book with.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. Our Refund & Cancellation Policy explains when you can reschedule or get a refund.

Is Mentra an emergency service?

No. If you or someone else is in danger, call 112.
