We make good psychological care easier to reach.

Mentra is a counselling and therapy service built around one idea: asking for help should feel simple, private and human.

Our purpose

Support that fits into real life

Many people wait a long time before they speak to someone, because finding a psychologist feels confusing, slow or uncomfortable. We want to change that. At Mentra you can say what is on your mind in your own words and in your own language, meet a psychologist who suits you, and start from wherever you are.

Mentra is based in Kozhikode, Kerala, and works with people across India and abroad, entirely online.

What guides us

  • Listen first, without judgement.
  • Treat privacy as non-negotiable.
  • Be clear, honest and kind in everything we say.
How we work

A calm, careful approach

Matched, not assigned

We take your concerns, language and preferences into account, and you choose who to book with.

Professional standards

Our psychologists are licensed and registered. Their qualifications are shown on their profiles.

Clear boundaries

Mentra is not an emergency service, and our online assessment is not a diagnosis. We point you to urgent help when it is needed.

Our promise

What you can expect from us

Confidentiality

Your sessions are private, within the limits of the law and professional ethics.

Safety

We take risk seriously and will always point you towards urgent help when it is needed.

Your language

Care in the language you think and feel in.

Respect

Every person is treated with dignity, whatever they are going through.
