Signs it’s time to talk
1
The low mood
won’t lift
Sadness or worry most days, for two weeks or more.
2
Your sleep or
appetite changed
Sleeping or eating much more, or much less, than usual.
3
Nothing feels
enjoyable anymore
Things you used to love now feel flat or like effort.
4
Work, studies or
relationships suffer
It’s getting harder to focus, cope or connect.
5
You keep saying
“I’m fine”
…but there’s no one you can really talk to.
You don’t need
a crisis to start
therapy.
Seeing even one of these signs is a good reason to talk to a professional.
